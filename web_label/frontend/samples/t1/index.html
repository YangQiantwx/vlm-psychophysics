Model input & prompt

Video + text → one answer: LEFT or RIGHT.

Watch the video and focus on the bright and dark stripe pattern inside the central circular aperture. The background image and aperture remain stationary. Your task is to judge the horizontal direction in which the stripe pattern travels relative to that fixed background. Follow the pattern across successive moments throughout the video, rather than comparing only the first and last images. Stripe spacing and visibility may vary, but the judgment is always about the stripes, not the depicted scene, the aperture boundary, or a change in overall brightness. Decide whether the ongoing stripe motion is toward the left or toward the right. Give your final answer as exactly one uppercase word: LEFT or RIGHT.
